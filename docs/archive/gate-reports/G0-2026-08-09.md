## Why the gate fired on item 6 rather than grinding through 4 and 5

The pre-committed decision rule in `05-metrics.md` §2a deliberately ordered the cheapest test first —
"Gate 0: the spreadsheet kill test, run FIRST, costs nothing". It cost nothing because pi persists
per-message `usage` with `cacheRead`/`cacheWrite`/`cost` directly in its session JSONL, so 82 sessions of
real history (1,956 tool calls, $76.12 of spend) were already on disk. Filling M1–M7 and accepting three
ADRs before running a free test that could disqualify the whole thesis would have been the wrong order,
and the roadmap said so in advance.

## What survives this gate

- **`docs/probes/baseline/`** — a read-only, stdlib-only, zero-cost instrument any pi user can run
  against their own session history. It is also the park's wake-up call (ADR-0005's revisit triggers).
- **Transferable findings**, none of which are pi-specific: the break-even inequality `S > 11.5·c·C`; the
  head-of-prefix cache asymmetry (tool definitions are cheap to keep, expensive to change, because they
  precede system prompt and history in the cacheable prefix); and the central result that **tool
  definitions are the small term** — 4.5–12% of a request against a 114:1 cache read/write ratio.
- **A complete audit trail**: 13 assumptions with evidence, 24 risks with triggers, 5 ADRs, a landscape
  matrix current as of 2026-08-09, and a question bank whose ★★★ answers are all sourced.
